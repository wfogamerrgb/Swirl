History of icecream!

First of all icecream got reinvented over and over again without interaction in different cultures and locations In China, in 200 BCE, milk and rice was mixed and frozen, creating a delcious frozen dessert. Usually, the rich and powerful people throughout history had access to icecream exclusively. In Persia, 400 BCE, rose water, lime juice, and noodles, were combined and frozen, forming an ice cream like frozen dessert. This also happened in the middle east!

In 1843, Nancy M. Johnson made a handcranked ice cream machine! It made ice cream production more reliable and churned evenly. It was difficult to make ice cream before this invention Furthermore Refrigeration changed everything, which made ice cream cheaper and easier to product. These inventions turned ice cream from a luxury product to a common every day treat! By the way it is unknown who invented ice cream
